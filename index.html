Little Muslim Stories

Short animated stories for families. In each one a curious boy asks his Baba or Mama a real question, and they answer with an authentic hadith of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), quoted word for word with its reference.

What this app does

Little Muslim Stories is the channel owner's own publishing tool. It renders the channel's animated Shorts and uploads them to the owner's YouTube channel, using the YouTube Data API with the owner's permission. It is not a public service: the only account it signs in to is the channel owner's.

It requests two permissions: upload videos (to publish the channel's own Shorts) and read-only access (to confirm which channel it is about to upload to). It never reads, changes or deletes anything else on YouTube or in a Google account.
