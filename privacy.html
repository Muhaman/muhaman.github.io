Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy covers Little Muslim Stories, the channel owner's tool that uploads the channel's own animated Shorts to YouTube.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the Little Muslim Stories YouTube channel. The app is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google user data we access

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and ID, so the app can confirm the right channel before uploading, and to check a video's processing status.

The app does not access viewer data, comments, analytics, email or any other Google service.

How the data is used and stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only by the channel owner, either in a private file on the owner's computer or as an encrypted secret in the owner's private build environment. It is used solely to upload the owner's videos. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Limited Use

Little Muslim Stories' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Deleting the stored token removes all data the app holds. Nothing else is retained.

Contact

Questions: reach the channel owner through the Little Muslim Stories YouTube channel.
